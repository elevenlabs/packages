Offer to pick a voice call back up after a page navigation tears the widget down. With `resume-call-after-navigation` enabled, the next page reopens the widget showing the conversation so far and a "Resume talking?" button, and pressing it starts a call that carries on from those turns instead of greeting the visitor again. Off by default; text chats are unaffected.
